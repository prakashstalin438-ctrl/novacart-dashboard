# AI E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

Build a modern, responsive, AI-powered E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard for business owners, managers, and analysts to monitor sales performance, customer behavior, inventory, shipping, and overall business health in real time.

The dashboard should feel like a premium SaaS product similar to Shopify Analytics, Stripe Dashboard, Vercel Analytics, and Linear.

The application must be visually stunning, extremely fast, fully responsive, and include realistic sample data.

---

# Business Name

NovaCart

---

# Business Description

NovaCart is an online e-commerce platform selling products across multiple categories including Electronics, Fashion, Home & Living, Beauty, Sports, Grocery, and Accessories.

The dashboard allows business owners to monitor KPIs, analyze customer behavior, optimize inventory, track shipping, monitor revenue, and receive AI-generated business insights.
